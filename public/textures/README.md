# Earth assets

- `earth-day.webp`, `earth-night.webp`: Solar System Scope Earth textures, distributed in the official Three.js example at https://threejs.org/examples/webgpu_tsl_earth.html. Day resized to 2048px; night resized to 1024px; compressed to WebP.
- `earth-clouds.webp`: Blue channel of the same example's `earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096.jpg`, extracted, resized to 1024px, and compressed to WebP.
- Original texture author: Solar System Scope, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/. Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- `earth-fallback.webp`: NASA / Earth Observatory, Blue Marble 2002, https://science.nasa.gov/resource/blue-marble-2002/. Resized to 720px and compressed to WebP. Downloaded from https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/solar/2023/09/1/1-bluemarble_west.jpg.

The scene is decorative. Its lighting, cloud layer, and orbital ring are not live telemetry or to scale. Attribution is visible on the home page and expanded on `/data-sources`.
