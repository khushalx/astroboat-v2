# Asian Paints research paper

A complete undergraduate company study covering strategy, finance, marketing and sustainability, with 6,651 words of main text and 15 cited sources. Research cut-off: 1 October 2026.

## Published files

- Interactive reader: `/research/asian-paints/Asian_Paints_Interactive_Paper.html`
- Editable Word document: `/research/asian-paints/Asian_Paints_Research_Paper.docx`
- Financial data: `/research/asian-paints/financial-data.csv`

The interactive reader is self-contained and includes historical chart controls, CSV export, print support and an educational sensitivity calculator. Its Word download uses a relative link to the sibling document.

## Source material

`paper.md` holds the narrative, `references.json` holds bibliography entries and source locators, and `financial-data.json` holds the financial dataset. Citation markers in the Markdown use `[@KEY]`; the published versions contain resolved citations. The published HTML and Word document are the complete reading copies.

This is a secondary research case study. The paper distinguishes reported facts, calculations and analytical interpretations; it does not claim original interviews or surveys. Annual financial comparisons use consolidated figures and identify profit definitions. The sensitivity calculator is educational and is not a forecast or valuation.

## Validation

The document was rendered and all 20 pages visually reviewed. The interactive reader was checked in Chrome for chart selection, scenario calculations, reset, CSV export, internal anchors and responsive layouts at 320, 390, 700, 1024 and 1440 pixels.
